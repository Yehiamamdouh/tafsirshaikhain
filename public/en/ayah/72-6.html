Tafsir of Surah Al-Jinn 72:6

﴿وَأَنَّهُ كَانَ رِجَالٌ مِّنَ الْإِنسِ يَعُوذُونَ بِرِجَالٍ مِّنَ الْجِنِّ فَزَادُوهُمْ رَهَقًا﴾

Translation of the meaning

And there were men from mankind who sought refuge in men from the jinn, so they [only] increased them in burden [i.e., sin].
