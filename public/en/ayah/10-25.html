Tafsir of Surah Yunus 10:25

﴿وَاللَّهُ يَدْعُو إِلَىٰ دَارِ السَّلَامِ وَيَهْدِي مَن يَشَاءُ إِلَىٰ صِرَاطٍ مُّسْتَقِيمٍ﴾

Translation of the meaning

And Allāh invites to the Home of Peace [i.e., Paradise] and guides whom He wills to a straight path.

English Translation - Noor International Center — v1.1.2 · QuranEnc.com

The words of the two imams are given in their original Arabic, verbatim, with references.

Shaykh al-Islam Ibn Taymiyyah

Imam Ibn al-Qayyim
