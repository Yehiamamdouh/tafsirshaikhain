Tafsir of Surah An-Naml 27:65

﴿قُل لَّا يَعْلَمُ مَن فِي السَّمَاوَاتِ وَالْأَرْضِ الْغَيْبَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَمَا يَشْعُرُونَ أَيَّانَ يُبْعَثُونَ﴾

Translation of the meaning

Say, "None in the heavens and earth knows the unseen except Allāh, and they do not perceive when they will be resurrected."
