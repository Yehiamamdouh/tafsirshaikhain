Tafsir of Surah Al-Anfaal 8:25

﴿وَاتَّقُوا فِتْنَةً لَّا تُصِيبَنَّ الَّذِينَ ظَلَمُوا مِنكُمْ خَاصَّةً وَاعْلَمُوا أَنَّ اللَّهَ شَدِيدُ الْعِقَابِ﴾

Translation of the meaning

And fear a trial which will not strike those who have wronged among you exclusively, and know that Allāh is severe in penalty.
