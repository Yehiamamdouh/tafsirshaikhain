Tafsir of Surah Al-Qiyaama 75:17

﴿إِنَّ عَلَيْنَا جَمْعَهُ وَقُرْآنَهُ﴾

Translation of the meaning

Indeed, upon Us is its collection [in your heart] and [to make possible] its recitation.
