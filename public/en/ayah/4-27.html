Tafsir of Surah An-Nisaa 4:27

﴿وَاللَّهُ يُرِيدُ أَن يَتُوبَ عَلَيْكُمْ وَيُرِيدُ الَّذِينَ يَتَّبِعُونَ الشَّهَوَاتِ أَن تَمِيلُوا مَيْلًا عَظِيمًا﴾

Translation of the meaning

Allāh wants to accept your repentance, but those who follow [their] passions want you to digress [into] a great deviation.

English Translation - Noor International Center — v1.1.2 · QuranEnc.com

The words of the two imams are given in their original Arabic, verbatim, with references.
