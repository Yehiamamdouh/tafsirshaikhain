Tafsir of Surah Al-A'raaf 7:186

﴿مَن يُضْلِلِ اللَّهُ فَلَا هَادِيَ لَهُ وَيَذَرُهُمْ فِي طُغْيَانِهِمْ يَعْمَهُونَ﴾

Translation of the meaning

Whoever Allāh sends astray - there is no guide for him. And He leaves them in their transgression, wandering blindly.
