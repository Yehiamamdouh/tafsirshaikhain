Tafsir of Surah Al-Jumu'a 62:3

﴿وَآخَرِينَ مِنْهُمْ لَمَّا يَلْحَقُوا بِهِمْ وَهُوَ الْعَزِيزُ الْحَكِيمُ﴾

Translation of the meaning

And [to] others of them who have not yet joined them. And He is the Exalted in Might, the Wise.

English Translation - Noor International Center — v1.1.2 · QuranEnc.com

The words of the two imams are given in their original Arabic, verbatim, with references.
