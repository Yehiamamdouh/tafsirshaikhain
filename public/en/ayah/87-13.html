Tafsir of Surah Al-A'laa 87:13

﴿ثُمَّ لَا يَمُوتُ فِيهَا وَلَا يَحْيَىٰ﴾

Translation of the meaning

Neither dying therein nor living.

English Translation - Noor International Center — v1.1.2 · QuranEnc.com

The words of the two imams are given in their original Arabic, verbatim, with references.
