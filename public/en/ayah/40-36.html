Tafsir of Surah Al-Ghaafir 40:36

﴿وَقَالَ فِرْعَوْنُ يَا هَامَانُ ابْنِ لِي صَرْحًا لَّعَلِّي أَبْلُغُ الْأَسْبَابَ﴾

Translation of the meaning

And Pharaoh said, "O Hāmān, construct for me a tower that I might reach the ways -
