Tafsir of Surah Al-Hajj 22:12

﴿يَدْعُو مِن دُونِ اللَّهِ مَا لَا يَضُرُّهُ وَمَا لَا يَنفَعُهُ ذَٰلِكَ هُوَ الضَّلَالُ الْبَعِيدُ﴾

Translation of the meaning

He invokes instead of Allāh that which neither harms him nor benefits him. That is what is the extreme error.

English Translation - Noor International Center — v1.1.2 · QuranEnc.com

The words of the two imams are given in their original Arabic, verbatim, with references.
