Tafsir of Surah Aal-i-Imraan 3:17

﴿الصَّابِرِينَ وَالصَّادِقِينَ وَالْقَانِتِينَ وَالْمُنفِقِينَ وَالْمُسْتَغْفِرِينَ بِالْأَسْحَارِ﴾

Translation of the meaning

The patient, the true, the obedient, those who spend [in the way of Allāh], and those who seek forgiveness before dawn.

English Translation - Noor International Center — v1.1.2 · QuranEnc.com

The words of the two imams are given in their original Arabic, verbatim, with references.
