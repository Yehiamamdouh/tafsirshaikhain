Tafsir of Surah Al-Insaan 76:29

﴿إِنَّ هَٰذِهِ تَذْكِرَةٌ فَمَن شَاءَ اتَّخَذَ إِلَىٰ رَبِّهِ سَبِيلًا﴾

Translation of the meaning

Indeed, this is a reminder, so he who wills may take to his Lord a way.
