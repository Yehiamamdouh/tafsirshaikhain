Tafsir of Surah Yunus 10:32

﴿فَذَٰلِكُمُ اللَّهُ رَبُّكُمُ الْحَقُّ فَمَاذَا بَعْدَ الْحَقِّ إِلَّا الضَّلَالُ فَأَنَّىٰ تُصْرَفُونَ﴾

Translation of the meaning

For that is Allāh, your Lord, the Truth. And what can be beyond truth except error? So how are you averted?
