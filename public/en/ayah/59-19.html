Tafsir of Surah Al-Hashr 59:19

﴿وَلَا تَكُونُوا كَالَّذِينَ نَسُوا اللَّهَ فَأَنسَاهُمْ أَنفُسَهُمْ أُولَٰئِكَ هُمُ الْفَاسِقُونَ﴾

Translation of the meaning

And be not like those who forgot Allāh, so He made them forget themselves. Those are the defiantly disobedient.
