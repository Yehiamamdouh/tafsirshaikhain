Tafsir of Surah As-Sajda 32:17

﴿فَلَا تَعْلَمُ نَفْسٌ مَّا أُخْفِيَ لَهُم مِّن قُرَّةِ أَعْيُنٍ جَزَاءً بِمَا كَانُوا يَعْمَلُونَ﴾

Translation of the meaning

And no soul knows what has been hidden for them of comfort for eyes [i.e., satisfaction] as reward for what they used to do.
