Tafsir of Surah Qaaf 50:18

﴿مَّا يَلْفِظُ مِن قَوْلٍ إِلَّا لَدَيْهِ رَقِيبٌ عَتِيدٌ﴾

Translation of the meaning

He [i.e., man] utters no word except that with him is an observer prepared [to record].
