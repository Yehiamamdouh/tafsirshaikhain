Tafsir of Surah Maryam 19:59

﴿فَخَلَفَ مِن بَعْدِهِمْ خَلْفٌ أَضَاعُوا الصَّلَاةَ وَاتَّبَعُوا الشَّهَوَاتِ فَسَوْفَ يَلْقَوْنَ غَيًّا﴾

Translation of the meaning

But there came after them successors [i.e., later generations] who neglected prayer and pursued desires; so they are going to meet evil -
