Tafsir of Surah Az-Zumar 39:33

﴿وَالَّذِي جَاءَ بِالصِّدْقِ وَصَدَّقَ بِهِ أُولَٰئِكَ هُمُ الْمُتَّقُونَ﴾

Translation of the meaning

And the one who has brought the truth [i.e., the Prophet (ﷺ)] and [they who] believed in it - those are the righteous.

English Translation - Noor International Center — v1.1.2 · QuranEnc.com
