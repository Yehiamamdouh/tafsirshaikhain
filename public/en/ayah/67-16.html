Tafsir of Surah Al-Mulk 67:16

﴿أَأَمِنتُم مَّن فِي السَّمَاءِ أَن يَخْسِفَ بِكُمُ الْأَرْضَ فَإِذَا هِيَ تَمُورُ﴾

Translation of the meaning

Do you feel secure that He who is above would not cause the earth to swallow you and suddenly it would sway?
