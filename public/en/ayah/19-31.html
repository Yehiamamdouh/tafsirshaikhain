Tafsir of Surah Maryam 19:31

﴿وَجَعَلَنِي مُبَارَكًا أَيْنَ مَا كُنتُ وَأَوْصَانِي بِالصَّلَاةِ وَالزَّكَاةِ مَا دُمْتُ حَيًّا﴾

Translation of the meaning

And He has made me blessed wherever I am and has enjoined upon me prayer and zakāh as long as I remain alive
