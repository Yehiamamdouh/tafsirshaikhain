Tafsir of Surah Al-Mujaadila 58:20

﴿إِنَّ الَّذِينَ يُحَادُّونَ اللَّهَ وَرَسُولَهُ أُولَٰئِكَ فِي الْأَذَلِّينَ﴾

Translation of the meaning

Indeed, the ones who oppose Allāh and His Messenger - those will be among the most humbled.

English Translation - Noor International Center — v1.1.2 · QuranEnc.com

The words of the two imams are given in their original Arabic, verbatim, with references.
