Tafsir of Surah Al-Anbiyaa 21:26

﴿وَقَالُوا اتَّخَذَ الرَّحْمَٰنُ وَلَدًا سُبْحَانَهُ بَلْ عِبَادٌ مُّكْرَمُونَ﴾

Translation of the meaning

And they say, "The Most Merciful has taken a son." Exalted is He! Rather, they are [but] honored servants.

English Translation - Noor International Center — v1.1.2 · QuranEnc.com

The words of the two imams are given in their original Arabic, verbatim, with references.

Shaykh al-Islam Ibn Taymiyyah

وأخبر أنهم عباد لله لا يشبهون به كما يشبه المعلول بالعلة والولد بالوالد«مجموع الفتاوى» 4/128

Imam Ibn al-Qayyim
