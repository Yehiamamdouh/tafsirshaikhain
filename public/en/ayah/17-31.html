Tafsir of Surah Al-Israa 17:31

﴿وَلَا تَقْتُلُوا أَوْلَادَكُمْ خَشْيَةَ إِمْلَاقٍ نَّحْنُ نَرْزُقُهُمْ وَإِيَّاكُمْ إِنَّ قَتْلَهُمْ كَانَ خِطْئًا كَبِيرًا﴾

Translation of the meaning

And do not kill your children for fear of poverty. We provide for them and for you. Indeed, their killing is ever a great sin.
