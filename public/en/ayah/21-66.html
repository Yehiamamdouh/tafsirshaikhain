Tafsir of Surah Al-Anbiyaa 21:66

﴿قَالَ أَفَتَعْبُدُونَ مِن دُونِ اللَّهِ مَا لَا يَنفَعُكُمْ شَيْئًا وَلَا يَضُرُّكُمْ﴾

Translation of the meaning

He said, "Then do you worship instead of Allāh that which does not benefit you at all or harm you?
