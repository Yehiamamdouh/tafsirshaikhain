Tafsir of Surah Yusuf 12:74

﴿قَالُوا فَمَا جَزَاؤُهُ إِن كُنتُمْ كَاذِبِينَ﴾

Translation of the meaning

They [the accusers] said, "Then what would be its recompense if you should be liars?"
