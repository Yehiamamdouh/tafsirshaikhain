Tafsir of Surah Maryam 19:19

﴿قَالَ إِنَّمَا أَنَا رَسُولُ رَبِّكِ لِأَهَبَ لَكِ غُلَامًا زَكِيًّا﴾

Translation of the meaning

He said, "I am only the messenger of your Lord to give you [news of] a pure boy [i.e., son]."

The words of the two imams are given in their original Arabic, verbatim, with references.
