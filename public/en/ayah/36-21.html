Tafsir of Surah Yaseen 36:21

﴿اتَّبِعُوا مَن لَّا يَسْأَلُكُمْ أَجْرًا وَهُم مُّهْتَدُونَ﴾

Translation of the meaning

Follow those who do not ask of you [any] payment, and they are [rightly] guided.
