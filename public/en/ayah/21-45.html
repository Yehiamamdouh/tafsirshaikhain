Tafsir of Surah Al-Anbiyaa 21:45

﴿قُلْ إِنَّمَا أُنذِرُكُم بِالْوَحْيِ وَلَا يَسْمَعُ الصُّمُّ الدُّعَاءَ إِذَا مَا يُنذَرُونَ﴾

Translation of the meaning

Say, "I only warn you by revelation." But the deaf do not hear the call when they are warned.

English Translation - Noor International Center — v1.1.2 · QuranEnc.com

The words of the two imams are given in their original Arabic, verbatim, with references.

Shaykh al-Islam Ibn Taymiyyah
