Tafsir of Surah Taa-Haa 20:55

﴿مِنْهَا خَلَقْنَاكُمْ وَفِيهَا نُعِيدُكُمْ وَمِنْهَا نُخْرِجُكُمْ تَارَةً أُخْرَىٰ﴾

Translation of the meaning

From it [i.e., the earth] We created you, and into it We will return you, and from it We will extract you another time.

English Translation - Noor International Center — v1.1.2 · QuranEnc.com

The words of the two imams are given in their original Arabic, verbatim, with references.
