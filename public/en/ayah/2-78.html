Tafsir of Surah Al-Baqara 2:78

﴿وَمِنْهُمْ أُمِّيُّونَ لَا يَعْلَمُونَ الْكِتَابَ إِلَّا أَمَانِيَّ وَإِنْ هُمْ إِلَّا يَظُنُّونَ﴾

Translation of the meaning

And among them are unlettered ones who do not know the Scripture except [indulgement in] wishful thinking, but they are only assuming.

English Translation - Noor International Center — v1.1.2 · QuranEnc.com
