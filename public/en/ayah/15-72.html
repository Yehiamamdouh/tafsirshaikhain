Tafsir of Surah Al-Hijr 15:72

﴿لَعَمْرُكَ إِنَّهُمْ لَفِي سَكْرَتِهِمْ يَعْمَهُونَ﴾

Translation of the meaning

By your life, [O Muḥammad], indeed they were, in their intoxication, wandering blindly.

English Translation - Noor International Center — v1.1.2 · QuranEnc.com

The words of the two imams are given in their original Arabic, verbatim, with references.

Imam Ibn al-Qayyim
