Tafsir of Surah Ad-Dhuhaa 93:3

﴿مَا وَدَّعَكَ رَبُّكَ وَمَا قَلَىٰ﴾

Translation of the meaning

Your Lord has not taken leave of you, [O Muḥammad], nor has He detested [you].

The words of the two imams are given in their original Arabic, verbatim, with references.
