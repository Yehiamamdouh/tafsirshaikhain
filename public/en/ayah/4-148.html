Tafsir of Surah An-Nisaa 4:148

﴿لَّا يُحِبُّ اللَّهُ الْجَهْرَ بِالسُّوءِ مِنَ الْقَوْلِ إِلَّا مَن ظُلِمَ وَكَانَ اللَّهُ سَمِيعًا عَلِيمًا﴾

Translation of the meaning

Allāh does not like the public mention of evil except by one who has been wronged. And ever is Allāh Hearing and Knowing.
