Tafsir of Surah An-Nisaa 4:133

﴿إِن يَشَأْ يُذْهِبْكُمْ أَيُّهَا النَّاسُ وَيَأْتِ بِآخَرِينَ وَكَانَ اللَّهُ عَلَىٰ ذَٰلِكَ قَدِيرًا﴾

Translation of the meaning

If He wills, He can do away with you, O people, and bring others [in your place]. And ever is Allāh competent to do that.
