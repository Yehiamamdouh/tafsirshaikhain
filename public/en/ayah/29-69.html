Tafsir of Surah Al-Ankaboot 29:69

﴿وَالَّذِينَ جَاهَدُوا فِينَا لَنَهْدِيَنَّهُمْ سُبُلَنَا وَإِنَّ اللَّهَ لَمَعَ الْمُحْسِنِينَ﴾

Translation of the meaning

And those who strive for Us - We will surely guide them to Our ways. And indeed, Allāh is with the doers of good.

English Translation - Noor International Center — v1.1.2 · QuranEnc.com

The words of the two imams are given in their original Arabic, verbatim, with references.
