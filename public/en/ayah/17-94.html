Tafsir of Surah Al-Israa 17:94

﴿وَمَا مَنَعَ النَّاسَ أَن يُؤْمِنُوا إِذْ جَاءَهُمُ الْهُدَىٰ إِلَّا أَن قَالُوا أَبَعَثَ اللَّهُ بَشَرًا رَّسُولًا﴾

Translation of the meaning

And what prevented the people from believing when guidance came to them except that they said, "Has Allāh sent a human messenger?"
